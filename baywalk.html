    PORAC BAYWALK PARK

    Porac Baywalk Park is a well-known, family-oriented linear park situated on General Luna Street in Barangay Cangatba, Porac, Pampanga. Set beside the tranquil Porac River, the park offers river views, playful massive sculptures (including a gorilla and the Incredible Hulk), and a lively nighttime food street with inexpensive local treats.

    Many residents affectionately call it Baywalk, named after the well-known Manila Baywalk, providing a peaceful place to relax and appreciate the beautiful river scenery—a great location for spending time with friends and family.
